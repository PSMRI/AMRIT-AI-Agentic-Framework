# AMRIT lifecycle context

## Stage

**STAGE 01/12 — Business & Product**

- Stage name: BRD
- Responsibility: Business Systems Analyst

## Actions taken

- Capture the business need from government partners, field teams, or support.
- Document as-is and to-be workflows.
- Define scope.
- Identify affected users.
- Produce functional specifications.
- Capture data mapping requirements.
- Capture report formula requirements where applicable.
- Review the BRD with the Product Manager and stakeholders.

## Inputs

- Business need or feature request
- Field feedback from ASHAs
- Facility staff feedback
- State operations feedback
- Support team inputs
- Government health programme guidelines, including RCH, NCD, TB, and Immunization
- Existing workflows and screenshots
- Meeting notes
- Jira issues
- Existing Confluence documentation

## Outputs

- Draft Business Requirement Document
- Inputs for an FRD
- Use-case and workflow information
- Mapping requirements where applicable

## Tools

- Confluence
- Excel
- dbdiagram.io

## Exit criterion

The lifecycle exit criterion is a BRD reviewed and signed off by the Product Manager. This skill does not complete or claim that criterion; it produces **Draft — Pending Human Review** for review by the Business Systems Analyst, Product Manager, and relevant stakeholders.
