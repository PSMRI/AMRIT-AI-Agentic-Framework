# Information checklist

Collect only items relevant to the current BRD. Not every item is required.

- Feature or change title
- Requesting stakeholder or team
- Business problem and reason for change
- Current and desired processes
- Affected AMRIT application and module
- Affected roles, states, or deployments
- Government programme
- In-scope and out-of-scope items
- Business and validation rules
- Notifications
- Audit and authorization requirements
- Data fields and mapping requirements
- Report and formula requirements
- Screenshots and workflows
- External and downstream systems
- Dependencies, constraints, and risks
- Acceptance criteria
- Open decisions
- Expected reviewers and approvers

## Example clarification questions

Ask only unanswered, necessary questions in small groups:

- What business problem is being solved, and who requested the change?
- Who performs the process today, and what happens?
- What should happen after the change?
- Which AMRIT module and user roles are affected?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- Which business and validation rules already exist?
- Are notifications, authorization, or audit logging required?
- Are reports, mappings, formulas, or downstream systems affected?
- Are government programme rules applicable?
- What decisions remain open?
